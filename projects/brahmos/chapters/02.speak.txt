[a documentary narrator explaining to a friend, warm and clear, steady pace] The name comes from two rivers, India's Brahmaputra and Russia's Moskva, because the two countries agreed to build it together in nineteen ninety-eight.
It's about eight metres long and weighs around three thousand kilograms, so it's nearly as long as a city bus.
Inside are five sections, and at the very front is the Seeker, its eye, followed by the Guidance System, its brain, and then the Warhead.
Behind them sits the Ramjet Engine, which keeps it fast, and at the back is the Booster, which gets it off the ground.
