Eighty-five thousand rupees, for a tiny gold device
that you stick on your temple
Pre-orders opened, and the website crashed
Doctors called it an expensive toy
It is Temple, built by Zomato’s founder, Deepinder Goyal
who put in $25 million of his own money
The promise: an estimate of the blood reaching your brain
It sends green, red and infrared light into your skin
Blood absorbs some of it, and the rest returns to a sensor
and an algorithm turns that into a Brain Flow score
But here is the catch: the artery under Temple feeds your skin
while your brain gets blood by a different route
So the real question: whose blood?
Study one: 20 people, a match of about 0.8
Study two: 59 of 60 sessions pointed the same way
But both studies are still preprints
Doctors: the anatomy, and a third of the signal unexplained
Only young, healthy people, and no medical approval
Goyal himself says: fitness and wellness, not medical
The gravity theory: standing upright means less blood to the brain
Doctors call it pseudoscience
So for now, not a big discovery
Not just a toy, but an experiment you can buy
